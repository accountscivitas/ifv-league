Price against points

Points per dollar, joining auction prices to weekly scoring. Keepers are included: a cheap contract carried for years is value delivered in each of those years. Started is what actually scored for the team; Total adds points scored while benched, which the player earned and the owner chose not to use. Per $ uses Total. Bench points exist only from 2018, and 2026 is absent because no points have been scored yet — a chart cannot tell a bust from a player who has not been given the chance to be one.
