Every contract and every draft pick in league history — every player who has ever been on an IFV roster, searchable. Click a player to see his whole IFV record; click a column heading to sort. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Pts is every point the player scored for that franchise that season, starting or on the bench. Bench scoring exists only from 2018 — 2011–2017 return starters alone, so an earlier total is a starters-only total and is not comparable with a later one. G counts weeks he was rostered and carried a score.

Auction dollars and FAAB dollars are not the same money. A draft price is bid against the $225 auction budget; a free-agent pickup is bid against the separate $275 FAAB budget. The 2020 Buccaneers D/ST cost $85 — absurd at auction, ordinary as a FAAB bid — and league rules then make that bid his cap salary. The Acquired column says which kind of dollar each row is.
