The league that almost was

15 championships won. 28 more that hung on a single decision. Four owners have never won anything, and each was one decision away.

championship won   his own lineup would have done it   he also needed a different draw   the number in a ring is how many start/sits it would have taken — an empty ring is none at all. Click any ring for the round-by-round route.

Every hollow ring is a COMPLETE route to the trophy: each round is either one he actually won or one a stated number of legal start/sits wins, and a round no lineup can win disqualifies the season rather than being drawn. Rounds a team did not reach use what it really scored that week in the consolation bracket — a real lineup, in a game that no longer mattered.
